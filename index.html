Request to borrow

  • Library staff will confirm the availability of the book and arrange a collection time with you by email within five business days.
  • When collecting the book, you must bring your student ID card or another form of photo identification, such as a passport.
  • You are responsible for taking proper care of the borrowed book and returning it by the due date.
  • You may be liable for any loss, damage, or applicable charges associated with the book.
